## Exact measurement protocol

- Upstream: [MiaAI-Lab/sparkDash](https://github.com/MiaAI-Lab/sparkDash), pinned
  `9e3f01729a4e63dccc2b6b608e9e67d3168891b6`.
- Original prose and distinct Python-code prompts, stock warmups, random salts,
  stream accounting and prefill calibration. Collectors are unchanged.
- Thinking **OFF**, temperature **0**, top_p **1**, **400 completion tokens** per
  stream. Serving outside this benchmark still defaults to MAX reasoning.
- Prose → code → 1K/2K/4K/8K prefill, three samples per prefill target.
- Aggregate decode = sum of post-first-token decode tokens divided by the shared
  first-token-to-last-token window. A 400-token stream contributes 399 decode tokens.
- Stream decode = its post-first-token tokens / its own decode interval. Posters
  report the median across requests, alongside the original aggregate.
- TTFT = request-to-first-token, **including prefill**. Short warm prompts can have
  very small TTFT. It is not an isolated post-prefill GPU timing measurement.
- Best measured aggregate is the maximum **complete cell** rate, not an
  instantaneous one-second monitor peak or a complete-workflow rate.

306/306 measured decode streams completed across the three runs, plus 36 prefill
samples. Counter deltas cover each entire stock campaign, including warmup and
prefill, and exclude the subsequent image investigation. Global counters also
include other proposal paths; MTP depth counters count the MTP-head chain only.

prove more absolute accepted tokens or a causal learning-only speedup. Cache and
warmup effects are also present. The weights are never trained by this learner.
